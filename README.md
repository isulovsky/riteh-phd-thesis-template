A professional LaTeX template for doctoral theses at the University of Rijeka, 
Faculty of Engineering (RITEH).

Includes bilingual title pages (English & Croatian), pre-configured sections, 
nomenclature support, and standard academic formatting.

**Unofficial template** – created as a reference for PhD candidates. 
Always verify with Faculty rules (if they are renewed) before final submission.
